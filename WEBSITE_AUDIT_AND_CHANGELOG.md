# TAJ Engineering & Project Services — Version 4

## Strategic change

Version 4 repositions the website around a specific client problem: owners and developers may lack independent visibility between issued project information and what is actually happening on site.

## Major changes

- Replaced the generic hero proposition with **“Catch construction problems before they are covered up.”**
- Added a six-case, source-backed evidence carousel covering incidents in Oyo, Abuja, Lagos, Kano, Plateau and Rivers.
- Added direct links to every original report.
- Added expandable **“What would TAJ check?”** controls to translate each case into a practical project-control question without claiming TAJ investigated the incident.
- Added prominent editorial caveats distinguishing reported facts from technical conclusions.
- Reorganised the client journey around project stage rather than generic service categories.
- Rebuilt the six core services around pre-construction readiness, construction-stage monitoring, materials/workmanship controls, condition screening, handover close-out and remote-owner monitoring.
- Rebuilt sample deliverables to match those six services.
- Added official COREN and CORBON verification links as part of project-readiness education.
- Updated the enquiry form and generated brief to match the new service structure.
- Preserved explicit boundaries around structural certification, statutory responsibilities and regulated professional opinions.

## Evidence-card design

The website uses original TAJ-designed source-summary graphics rather than copying full news pages. Each graphic identifies the source, location, date, headline and a short attributed summary, with a direct link to the publisher.
