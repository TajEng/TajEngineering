Privacy notice

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Information submitted through the website

The project-brief form is processed in the visitor’s browser. It prepares a summary for copying, downloading or sending by email. The website does not store the form submission in a database.

Email and WhatsApp communication

The generated brief can be sent by email or opened in WhatsApp. Email is used to understand the enquiry, prepare a response and maintain relevant business correspondence. WhatsApp messages are delivered and stored through WhatsApp/Meta's own platform under its terms and privacy policy, outside TAJ's website. Do not send sensitive personal or financial information unless it is necessary and an appropriate channel has been agreed.

Website files and analytics

This static website does not require an account. Hosting providers may retain technical access logs under their own policies. Analytics should be added only with an updated notice where required.
