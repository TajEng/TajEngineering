# TAJ Website Evidence Sources

The website's evidence carousel summarises the following public reports. TAJ does not claim to have investigated any of the incidents. The original sources remain the authority for the statements attributed to them.

1. **Ibadan, Oyo State — 2 June 2025**  
   Channels Television — *Oyo Seals Off Collapsed Building Under Construction*  
   https://www.channelstv.com/2025/06/02/oyo-seals-off-collapsed-building-under-construction/  
   Website takeaway: Oyo officials said some materials used were sub-standard and of low quality and announced integrity testing.

2. **Jikwoyi, Abuja — 7 April 2026**  
   Channels Television — *Wike Orders Arrest, Land Seizure Over Abuja Building Collapse*  
   https://www.channelstv.com/2026/04/07/wike-orders-arrest-land-seizure-over-abuja-building-collapse/  
   Website takeaway: FCT authorities said the building had no construction approval and that repeated stop-work notices had been ignored.

3. **Maryland, Lagos — 2024**  
   Lagos State Building Control Agency — *Maryland building collapse: Lagos to prosecute owner, site engineer*  
   https://lasbca.lagosstate.gov.ng/maryland-building-collapse-lagos-to-prosecute-owner-site-engineer/  
   Website takeaway: LASBCA said the abandoned project required recertification before work resumed and that notices for non-destructive testing and permit revalidation were ignored.

4. **Sabon Gari, Kano — 14 July 2025**  
   Channels Television — *Two Dead, Eight Rescued As Uncompleted Building Collapses In Kano*  
   https://www.channelstv.com/2025/07/14/two-dead-eight-rescued-as-uncompleted-building-collapses-in-kano/  
   Website takeaway: the head of Kano SEMA described the structure as inferior and called for action against building with inferior materials.

5. **Jos, Plateau State — 13 July 2024**  
   Reuters — *Nigerian school building collapses killing 22 people*  
   https://www.reuters.com/world/africa/nigerian-school-building-collapses-killing-22-people-sky-news-says-2024-07-13/  
   Website takeaway: 22 people were reported killed. The website does not state a final technical cause for this incident.

6. **Port Harcourt, Rivers State — 24 June 2026**  
   Channels Television — *One Dead, Three Rescued As Building Under Construction Collapses*  
   https://www.channelstv.com/2026/06/24/four-storey-building-collapses-in-port-harcourt-rescue-efforts-underway/  
   Website takeaway: an active construction site collapsed, one person was reported dead, three were rescued, and the site was subsequently sealed.
